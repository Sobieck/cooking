96 g olive oil

1 bag frozen chopped onions

36 g minced garlic

12 g chicken bouillon 

6.5 cups water

630 g uncooked brown rice

12 g salt

2 15 ounce can of black beans

2 bunch of cilantro

1.25 g ground black pepper

2 lime, cut into wedges

## Directions

Adjust oven rack to middle position; heat oven to 375°F. Heat oil in large Dutch oven over medium heat until shimmering. Add pepper and cook, stirring occasionally, until well browned, 12 to 14 minutes. Stir in garlic until fragrent, 30 seconds or so

Add broth and water; cover and bring to boil. Remove pot from heat; stir in rice and salt. Cover and bake rice until tender, 65 to 70 minutes.

Remove pot from oven, uncover, fluff rice with fork, stir in peppers, and replace lid; let stand 5 minutes. Stir in cilantro and black pepper. Serve, passing lemon wedges separately.
