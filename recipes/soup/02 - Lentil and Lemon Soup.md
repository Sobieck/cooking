LENTIL SOUP WITH CUMIN, CORIANDER AND LEMON ZEST

384 g lentils

16 cups water

2 Bay leaf

2 medium yellow onion, diced

48 g olive oil

24 g garlic, minced

4 stalks celery, diced

2.4 g freshly ground coriander

4 g freshly ground cumin

Peel of 1 lemon, minced

Salt

fresh greens

### instructions

Sort through lentils for stones or other debris. Combine in soup pot with water and bay leaf. Bring to boil. Reduce heat, cover and simmer 30 to 45 minutes until lentils are tender.

Add onion, garlic, celery. Continue cooking 30 to 40 minutes until vegetables are tender. Stir in coriander, cumin and lemon zest. Season to taste with salt. Serve hot. Makes 4 to 6 servings.
